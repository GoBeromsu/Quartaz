I realized that youth is beautiful.
How fresh it is, and how fresh it must have been.

At times it may look as if it is fading,
but one day even the steps backward will be seen as dance.

Perhaps young people do not know their own beauty
because they have been endlessly beautiful all along.

One day, when they set that freshness down by choice
and lift it into maturity, they will finally see and know.
