## About Me

Hello, I'm Beomsu Koh, an AI engineer.

I build and run products through agent-driven development, publish open-source tools for Obsidian and personal knowledge systems, and teach what I learn along the way. Alongside product work, I pursue first-author software-engineering research on tangled code commits.

### Missions

All technology amplifies intent. I believe good intent makes the world better.

I want to stop attention theft and the seizure of people's cognitive resources, and help people stay truly focused on what matters to them.

## Work

- **SeeON** (2026.06 – 2026.10, Senior AI Lab, co-founder and CTO): real-time fall detection. With DeepStream and TensorRT, one RTX 5070 Ti handled 13 real cameras at 29.97 fps each, 389.6 fps in total.
- **[OSS Hub](https://jnu-oss-hub.com/)** (2026.07 – now, Chonnam National University, Developer & PM): a platform that tracks students' open-source records.
- **KREN AI Ops** (2026.06 – 2026.08, freelance for KREN, sole designer and builder; project name SICHIMI AI Ops): moved KREN's GitOps-centred network operations to a Hermes agent team working through MCP.
