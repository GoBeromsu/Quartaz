I’m pleased to share that I’ve graduated with an MSc in Advanced Computer Science from The University of Sheffield, awarded with Distinction.

Pursuing an MSc in the UK was a deliberate decision to immerse myself in an unfamiliar environment—new language, new culture, and high academic expectations. This experience reshaped my approach to learning, problem-solving, and sustained effort under uncertainty.

The program involved close collaboration with peers from diverse backgrounds, demanding technical modules, and a dissertation that required long-term focus and rigor. I encountered failure frequently, and through these challenges, I learned that confidence is built not from outcomes, but from consistently showing up and adjusting.

I’m especially grateful to my supervisors, Neil Walkinshaw and Donghwan Shin.  
From Neil, I learned to start at the high level—to frame problems before rushing to solutions.  
When I asked Donghwan about the meaning of the MSc, he said it would help me understand myself better. Looking back, that proved true. I’m sincerely thankful for their guidance and supervision.

I really loved this period.

As I move forward, I’m excited to explore the AI era—building products and experimenting with how agents solve complex problems.

This MSc was not only an academic milestone but also a deliberate slingshot—placing me on a trajectory where growth was unavoidable.

I welcome connections with others building in this space :)
